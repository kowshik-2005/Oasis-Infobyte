Biography

Steve Paul Jobs was an American entrepreneur, inventor, and visionary leader. He was the co-founder and CEO of Apple Inc., a company that revolutionized personal computing, smartphones, music, and digital publishing.

What I admire most about Steve Jobs is his relentless pursuit of perfection. He believed in simplicity, innovation, and thinking differently. His ability to combine technology with creativity transformed industries and inspired millions of people around the world.

Jobs once said, "Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish." This mindset reflects his curiosity, courage, and determination to create products that truly changed how we live and work.

Made with admiration and inspiration.
